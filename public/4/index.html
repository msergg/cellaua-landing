The list that
keeps up.

Shared with your family. Aware of where you are.

One list, everyone

Written on any phone. Seen on all of them.

Reminds you outside

Decided on your phone. Location stays there.

Two shops, two lists

Timber never shows up at the supermarket.

Remembers the weekly

Clearing keeps it. Two taps to add it back.

What a month actually costs

Counted from your own shopping, and honest about what it could not price.

Cella — Latin for the storeroom at the back of the house.

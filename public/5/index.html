Cella

Buy it
together.

One shopping list for the whole family.

It tells you outside the shop, not after.

The decision happens on your phone. Your location never leaves it.

It remembers what you always buy.

Clearing the list keeps it. Adding it back takes two taps.

And what a month actually cost.

From your own shopping — and honest about what it could not price.

Cella — Latin for the storeroom at the back of the house.
